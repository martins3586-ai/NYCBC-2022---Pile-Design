Working

NYC BC 1810.3.1: allowable geotechnical capacity uses a factor of safety of at least 2 and must be shown by load tests, except driven piles of 40 tons or less. The static analysis follows FHWA GEC-12 (FHWA-NHI-16-009) using chart values digitized in the FHWA DRIVEN program; it is an estimate for planning and does not replace load tests. Table 1810.4.1.3 limits may be exceeded only where substantiated by load tests and analysis (1810.4.1.5.1).
